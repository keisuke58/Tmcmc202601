\noindent Thank you very much, Szymon and Rumjhum, for the careful review of the version circulated on 17 September.
Below, each point is listed with how it was handled in the new manuscript.
Because the paper is now prepared for \textit{Extreme Mechanics Letters} (fewer than 4000 words, at most six figures), it is a shorter version that focuses on the modelling and inference framework; several items of the previous version (UMAP, cross-condition transfer, phase-correlation table) are therefore no longer part of the paper.
Section numbers refer to the new manuscript; ``S'' refers to the Supplementary Material.
Two changes go beyond your comments and are summarised first, because several answers below depend on them.

\paragraph{New since 25 September: measured data and a two-channel likelihood.}
The calibration now uses the measured replicate values that Nils provided (three to five biological replicates per time point) instead of values read off the published box plots, which deviate from them by up to 0.42 in a species fraction in the dysbiotic conditions (Section~3, Section~S10).
The viable (PMA) and the total-count compositions are compared separately with their model counterparts, the living fractions $\phi_i\psi_i/\sum_j\phi_j\psi_j$ and the volume fractions $\phi_i/\sum_j\phi_j$, and the community live fraction from LIVE/DEAD staining with $\sum_i\phi_i\psi_i/\sum_i\phi_i$ (Eq.~5).
This constrains the species viabilities $\psi_i$ species by species and is why all runs were repeated once more.
As agreed with Nils, the data are cited and stated as available from the authors on request; they are not redistributed with the paper or the code archive.

\paragraph{New since 25 September: strict acceptance criteria.}
Every stage is accepted only after five criteria (a)--(e) with three seeds (Section~3).
For criterion (d), the agreement of the maximum log-likelihood across seeds, each seed's value is the highest found by a local optimisation from its best particles.
Where a group fails a criterion, this is reported as it is in Table~S5 rather than by relaxing the criterion afterwards: the DS stage-(ii) group fails (d) because one seed found a higher optimum than the other two.
In DH, the final stage on the narrowed box (stage iv) fails criterion (e): the three seeds settle in different modes (seed medians of $a_{13}$, $a_{22}$, $a_{24}$ up to 0.8 posterior SD apart).
Stage (iii), which uses the same likelihood on the wider box and meets all criteria, is therefore the final stage for DH, and the mode split is stated as a limitation (Section~4.5).

\paragraph{New since 25 September: what changed in the dysbiotic flow reactor (DH).}
With the measured data, the ratio of the Pg fraction on day 21 to day 15 is 14.9 (viable cells), not 2.7 as read off the published figure.
The model reproduces this late increase (posterior median 11--13 per seed), but the data do not determine its size (90\% intervals from 0.6 to 32; the seed MAPs give 9.6, 13.5 and 18.0 at nearly equal likelihood).
The in-silico knockout is therefore weaker than in the version of 17 September: removing \textit{F.~nucleatum} lowers the ratio from about 12 to 3.0 (90\% interval 1.0--4.8), i.e.\ it reduces the increase about fourfold but does not abolish it; setting only the Fn--Pg coefficient to zero removes it in most samples.
Abstract, highlights and cover letter now say ``reduces about fourfold''.
The pH check now uses the continuous pH record: in DH the predicted pH follows the measured rise from about 6.4 to 7.0 ($R^2=0.71$, 85\% of the measured points inside the 90\% band); in CH the measured pH stays near 6.3 and the prediction matches this level but not its small variations.

\paragraph{General point: effective parameters vs.\ biological interactions.}
We follow this throughout: the coefficients $a_{ij}$ are introduced as \emph{effective, undirected interactions of the model} that lump unresolved mechanisms (Section 2), all biological readings are phrased as consistency with the literature, never as demonstration of a mechanism, and a new analysis states explicitly which coefficients the data determine at all (Fig.~3, Table~S7).

\section*{Points that required computation}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l Y l@{}}
\toprule
B1 & \emph{Same data used three times (narrowing the prior).} Narrowed boxes are the stage posterior mean $\pm4$ standard deviations, which removes less than $10^{-4}$ of a Gaussian marginal; when the viability channel is added the inference returns to the full box, and any bound at which posterior mass accumulates is widened and the stage repeated (Section 3, Table S4). To check that starting stage (iii) partly from the stage-(ii) result does not bias it, one CH stage-(iii) run was started entirely from the prior: its maximum log-likelihood lies within the range of the three warm-started seeds and its medians and 90\% bounds within 0.03 of theirs (Section~3). A full comparison with a single inference on the wide box only is not shown. & \partly\\
B2 & \emph{Sparsity / identifiability not demonstrated.} New: every entry is classified as identified, sign-only or not identified from its posterior spread relative to the prior box (Fig.~3, Section~S6, Tables~S6 and S7), every stage is repeated with three seeds that must agree, and the number of directions in which the likelihood itself is narrow at the MAP (Fisher information) is reported as an independent check (Section~4.2, Section~S6). A recovery test was added (Section~S7): synthetic data generated from a posterior point of each condition with the measured noise, inferred again with the same pipeline, reproduce the classification in 14, 15, 14 and 15 of the 15 entries (CS, CH, DS, DH); the intervals of the identified entries are, however, too narrow in CH (2 of 6 contain the truth), which is stated as a limitation. & \done\\
B3 & \emph{No convergence diagnostics for Phase 2.} Every stage, including the final one, is run with three independent seeds and accepted only after explicit criteria (log-likelihood recomputation, tempering to $\beta=1$, moves per particle and per dimension, agreement of $\max\ln L$ after local optimisation and of the medians across seeds; Section~3, Table~S5). CS and CH meet all criteria in every stage. In DS, stage (ii) fails criterion (d) and is reported as such; stages (iii) and (iv) meet all criteria. In DH, stage (iv) fails criterion (e) (seeds in different modes); stage (iii), which meets all criteria, is the final stage and the split is reported as a limitation. The exploratory stage (i) only sets the stage-(ii) box; the Bayes factors are computed from separate stage-(i) runs with more mutation steps that meet all criteria. & \done\\
B4 & \emph{Independent Gaussian errors for compositional data.} Stated as a limitation (Section~4.5: intervals may be somewhat too narrow). The standard deviations are now estimated from the spread of the measured replicates per species and time point (interquartile range/1.35, at least 0.05), for each composition separately. & \done\\
B5 & \emph{Model-to-data mapping implicit.} Section~3 now states which model quantity is compared with which measurement (living fractions with the viable composition, volume fractions with the total-count composition, the community live fraction with LIVE/DEAD staining), the renormalisation over the five species, the time mapping (time since inoculation, Day 1 as initial state, with the sensitivity to starting at Day 1) and the likelihood weights, with their effect in Section~S9. & \done\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
