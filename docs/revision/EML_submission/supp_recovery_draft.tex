\section{Recovery test on synthetic data}
\label{sec:S_recovery}
To test whether the pipeline recovers known interaction coefficients and whether the classification of Section~\ref{sec:S_ident} reflects the information in the data rather than the sampler, we generated synthetic data from the final MAP of CH (seed with the highest maximum log-likelihood) at the measured time points, with Gaussian noise of the same standard deviations as in the likelihood (viable and total-count compositions and live fraction), and inferred them again with the stage-(iv) settings and three seeds.
The log-likelihood of the true parameters on the synthetic data, $-20.95$, lies at the 72nd percentile of its distribution over noise realisations, so the realisation is typical.

Because the truth is a single point, the coverage of the 90\% intervals is not an independent 0.9 trial per entry (entries are correlated, and Bayesian coverage is an average over the prior); the claim rests on the agreement of the classification.
